Help with your app

A rhythm that fits your life.

Begin with a small step

Choose a routine, set a time, and make the duration your own. A few meaningful minutes can be a good place to begin.

Choose your approach

Use flexible scheduling when plans change, or a strict focus session when you want to keep the time you set aside.

Give your attention room

Set up app blocking and choose the apps you need to keep available. Your phone’s permissions make protection possible.

Personal, calm, and yours.

Plan routines, add them to your calendar, choose a comfortable light or dark appearance, and look back on the time you have made. Your private reflections stay on your device.
